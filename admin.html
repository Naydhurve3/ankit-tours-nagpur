How Private/Public Works

  • Owner portal: Changes made while connected to Neon save through protected owner APIs and appear across devices.
  • Public website: Visitors receive only items where Visible is enabled. Hidden items remain available to the owner.
  • Images: Use accurate, approved vehicle photographs. URL-based images are preferred until managed media storage is added.
  • Bookings and drivers: Customer and driver information is private and must never be copied into public content fields.
  • Fallback mode: Local/JSON controls are for development recovery only and are not a replacement for cloud publishing.
