## Owner additions and display

New records use `custom-ID`, are attached to an existing category, and use owner-entered English/Marathi names, price, note, order, pin and visibility. All enquiry buttons pass that exact service to the form. No service is silently dropped when a category is collapsed; it remains searchable and becomes visible when expanded.
